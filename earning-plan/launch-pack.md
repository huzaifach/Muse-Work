# First-Income Launch Pack
**For:** complete beginner, small city Pakistan, zero sellable skills (assumed) — everything below is copy-paste ready.
**Date:** 2026-10-17 | **Strategy:** dual track — local cash (fastest money in hand) + Fiverr pipeline (online, slower but scalable).

> **Honest expectations (read once):** Nothing here guarantees income. Local work typically pays in days/weeks; Fiverr's first order usually takes 2–4 weeks of active promotion, and new sellers wait **14 days** after order completion before funds clear. This pack removes every "I don't know where to start" excuse — the rest is daily effort.

> **Before publishing gigs:** spend 2–3 days practicing each gig's core task with free YouTube tutorials (search: "data entry practice Excel", "virtual assistant tutorial", "PDF to Excel conversion"). You don't need to be expert — you need to be reliable.

## 1. Fiverr Gig Drafts — ONE lane: Data Entry / Virtual Assistance

**Why this lane:** needs no degree, no portfolio, no software purchases. A computer + internet + carefulness is enough. All 3 gigs below are in the same lane so your profile looks focused, not scattered.

**General rules for all gigs:**
- Publish in this order: Gig 1 → Gig 2 → Gig 3 (one per day is fine).
- New gigs take **7–14 days** to gain search impressions — don't panic in week 1.
- Reply to every message **within the hour** — response time affects your ranking.
- Your first 5 reviews decide everything: price low, over-deliver, be polite.
- Fiverr takes a **flat 20% fee** on every order. ($5 order → you keep $4.)
- ⚠️ Do NOT follow old tutorials about a "Buyer Requests" board — that board is **discontinued**. Buyers now reach you through **AI-matched Briefs** (the feature name keeps changing — check the current name inside your Fiverr app) and through gig search. Ignore any pre-2024 guide that tells you to refresh Buyer Requests.

## 5. Setup Checklists (exact steps)

### ✅ CHECKLIST A — Payoneer account (Day 1–2)

Payoneer is how Fiverr money reaches you in Pakistan. Verification takes days — **start this FIRST**, before anything else.

- [ ] Go to **payoneer.com** → Sign Up → **Individual** account
- [ ] Enter your real name **exactly as on your CNIC** (mismatch = blocked withdrawals later)
- [ ] Email + strong password (save in a safe place)
- [ ] Enter CNIC details when asked; upload clear CNIC photos if requested
- [ ] Add your Pakistani bank account details (any local bank works)
- [ ] Submit and wait for verification email (usually **1–3 days** — check spam folder too)
- [ ] ⚠️ **PayPal does NOT support receiving money in Pakistan** — don't build any plan around PayPal payouts.

*Fees to know (always confirm the exact figure shown in-app before withdrawing — they vary): incoming ~1% over $100 ($1 flat under $100); annual fee $29.95 if yearly turnover is under $2,000 (waived above).*

### ✅ CHECKLIST B — Link JazzCash to Payoneer (inside the JazzCash app)

Official partnership (Sept 2026): instant transfers, minimum ~$1.

- [ ] Open the **JazzCash app** (account must be biometric-verified/active)
- [ ] Find the **Payoneer** option in the app menu

- [ ] ⚠️ The **name + CNIC on JazzCash and Payoneer MUST match exactly** — otherwise linking fails

- [ ] Fee is roughly **2–3%** — the app shows the exact fee before you confirm; read it every time.

## 6. Scam-Protection One-Pager ⚠️ READ THIS FIRST — NON-NEGOTIABLE

**Why this is here:** a desperate beginner is the #1 target for scammers in Pakistan. Real cases from 2026: WhatsApp "task" scams, Rs 500 fake interview fees (NCCIA bust, Sept 2026), fake trading apps (one victim lost Rs 2.779M). **If an "opportunity" finds YOU first — especially on WhatsApp/Telegram — assume it's a scam until proven otherwise.**

### The 6 Golden Rules (6 Sunheri Usool)

**1. Kabhi paise de kar kaam mat lo. / NEVER pay money to get work.**
EN: Real clients pay YOU. Any "registration fee", "activation fee", "verification fee", or "deposit to unlock earnings" = scam. No exceptions.
UR: Asli client tumhe paise deta hai, tum se leta nahi. "Registration fee", "activation fee" ya "paise jama karwao phir kamai hogi" — 100% fraud hai.

**2. Baat hamesha platform par rakho. / Keep all client chat ON the platform.**
EN: On Fiverr/Upwork, never move conversation to WhatsApp/Telegram before an official order/contract exists. Scammers want you off-platform where there's no protection.
UR: Fiverr/Upwork par client se baat hamesha platform ke andar rakho. Jo WhatsApp par le jane par israar kare, samjho kuch garbar hai.

**3. Paise sirf official app ke andar verify karo. / Verify money ONLY inside the official app.**
EN: Screenshots of payments are faked in seconds. Open your JazzCash/Payoneer/bank app yourself and check the balance. No balance in the app = no payment received.
UR: Payment ke screenshot aasani se jaali banaye jate hain. Khud apni JazzCash/Payoneer/bank app kholo aur balance dekho. App me paise nahi = paise nahi aaye.

**4. "Guaranteed returns" hamesha jhooth hai. / Guaranteed profits = always a scam.**
EN: No trading app, "mentor group", or investment scheme guarantees 1–5% daily profit. That's mathematically impossible long-term — it's a trap.
UR: Koi trading app ya "mentor group" roz ka pakka munafa nahi de sakta. Ye na-mumkin hai — dhoka hai.

**5. Muft test / muft kaam nahi. / No free trials, no free work.**
EN: "Do this sample task free and we'll hire you" from strangers = your work stolen. Small paid test milestones only, inside the platform.
UR: Ajnabi ka "pehle muft me kaam karke dikhao" matlab tumhara kaam muft me le jana. Muft me kaam kabhi mat karo.

**6. OTP/PIN kisi ko mat do. / NEVER share OTPs or PINs.**
EN: No real bank, JazzCash, or Payoneer agent will EVER call asking for your OTP or PIN. Anyone who asks is a thief — hang up.
UR: Koi asli bank ya JazzCash wala kabhi call karke OTP/PIN nahi mangta. Jo mange, wo chor hai — call kaat do.

### 🚨 Agar dhoka ho gaya to / IF YOU GET SCAMMED:

1. **Ghabrao mat, foran action lo.** Saboot sambhalo: chat screenshots, numbers, transaction IDs, receipts.
2. **FIA Cyber Crime Wing ko report karo** — yehi Pakistan me online fraud ki official authority hai. Unka official complaint channel use karo (search: "FIA cybercrime complaint" — hamesha official website/app hi kholo, kisi ke bheje hue link par mat jao).
3. Apne bank / JazzCash / Easypaisa ko foran inform karo — wo transaction rokne ya trace karne me madad kar sakte hain.
4. **Sharminda mat ho.** Scammers isi sharam ka faida uthate hain taake tum report na karo. Report karna tumhe bhi bachata hai aur agle shikaar ko bhi.
